About MoneyBuddy

MoneyBuddy is a personal, non-commercial finance-organising application for Android. It is intended for the personal use of the application owner and is not offered as a banking, payment, investment, credit, or financial advice service.

Current application

The current version uses synthetic demonstration data and local device storage. It does not initiate payments, transfer money, or provide a live connection to a bank.

Future account-information connection

If a read-only Open Banking connection is enabled in a future version, access will require the user's consent through the selected Open Banking provider and the user's bank. MoneyBuddy will not collect or store the user's bank password and will not initiate payments or transfers.

Financial information

MoneyBuddy is an organisational tool only. Information shown by the app may be incomplete, delayed, or inaccurate. Users must verify balances, transactions, and financial decisions with their bank or an appropriately qualified professional.

Acceptable use

The application must not be used to attempt unauthorised access to a bank, bypass consent, initiate payments, or interfere with the Open Banking provider or bank systems.
