MoneyBuddy Privacy Policy

Last updated: 31 October 2026

About MoneyBuddy

MoneyBuddy is a personal finance organiser for Android. The current version uses synthetic demonstration data stored locally on the device.

Information in the current version

The current version does not connect to a bank and does not collect, transmit, or sell personal financial information. Demonstration transactions are stored locally on the device for testing the app.

Future Open Banking connection

If a read-only Open Banking connection is enabled in a future version, account and transaction information will be accessed only with the user's consent through the selected Open Banking provider. MoneyBuddy will not collect or store the user's bank password. Details about data storage, deletion, retention, and third-party processing will be updated before that feature is released.

Data deletion

The app will provide a way to delete locally stored financial data. Users may also uninstall the app to remove its local application data, subject to Android device settings and backups.
